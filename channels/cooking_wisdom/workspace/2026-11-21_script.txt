=== SUBTITLES & NARRATION SCRIPTS ===
[Slide 1]
  Subtitle: The secret Japanese seasoning order!
  Narration: Why do Japanese chefs season using a strict five-letter order named Sa-Shi-Su-Se-So?

[Slide 2]
  Subtitle: Sugar first, then salt.
  Narration: Sugar penetrates slowly, while salt draws out moisture and can block sugar if added first.

[Slide 3]
  Subtitle: Vinegar arrives near the end.
  Narration: Vinegar goes later, saving its sharp aroma from softening too much under long heat.

[Slide 4]
  Subtitle: Soy sauce and miso last!
  Narration: Soy sauce and miso must be added last, or heat destroys their delicate fragrances.

[Slide 5]
  Subtitle: Timing builds perfect flavor depth.
  Narration: Master the sequence, and your simmered dishes will carry unmatched depth and aroma.
