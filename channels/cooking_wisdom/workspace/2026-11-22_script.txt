  Narration: Peak-season frozen fish and vegetables actually beat out-of-season store produce in pure flavor.

[Slide 2]
  Subtitle: Wipe fish dry before you freeze.
  Narration: Salt fish with sake, wait ten minutes, then thoroughly wipe away odor-causing moisture.

[Slide 3]
  Subtitle: Frozen daikon absorbs flavor instantly!
  Narration: Freezing breaks daikon fibers, making it soak up rich simmered broth far faster.

[Slide 4]
  Subtitle: Microwave eggplant whole before freezing!
  Narration: Never freeze eggplant raw; microwave it whole first to keep its color and texture.

[Slide 5]
  Subtitle: Thaw in ice water, not counters!
  Narration: Thaw delicate fish in ice water for ten minutes, and cook clams straight frozen.
