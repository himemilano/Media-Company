# Media Company Philosophy

## Who We Are

Media Company exists to transform authentic Japanese knowledge into practical wisdom for people around the world.

We are not an entertainment company.

We are not a news company.

We are a knowledge media company.

Our mission is to uncover the thinking, culture, science, craftsmanship, and everyday habits that have shaped Japan, and explain them in a way that people from other cultures can understand and apply.

Every video should answer one important question:

"Why does Japan do it this way?"

---

# Our Mission

Our purpose is to build understanding between cultures.

Rather than simply introducing Japanese customs, we explain the values, history, psychology, education, science, and philosophy behind them.

Knowledge without context creates stereotypes.

Context creates understanding.

Understanding creates respect.

---

# Our Principles

Every piece of content must satisfy the following principles.

## 1. Teach, don't advertise.

We educate.

We never create promotional content disguised as education.

---

## 2. Explain causes, not only results.

Do not simply show that something exists.

Explain why it exists.

The "why" is always more valuable than the "what."

---

## 3. One video, one lesson.

Every Short should communicate one clear idea.

Avoid trying to teach multiple concepts in one video.

---

## 4. Build curiosity first.

Begin with a surprising question or observation.

Curiosity comes before explanation.

---

## 5. Respect facts.

Do not exaggerate.

Do not invent statistics.

Do not romanticize Japan.

Present balanced, factual explanations whenever possible.

---

## 6. Respect every culture.

The goal is not to claim Japan is superior.

The goal is to explain one different way of thinking that viewers may find useful.

---

## 7. Inspire practical action.

Every video should leave viewers with one practical insight they can apply in their own lives.

Knowledge should improve daily life.

---

# AI Content Standards

Every AI agent in this company must follow these rules.

Always prioritize:

- Truth
- Clarity
- Curiosity
- Practical value
- Cultural understanding

Never prioritize:

- Clickbait
- Rage bait
- Fear
- Political messaging
- National superiority
- Stereotypes

---

# Production Philosophy

Our videos are built around a simple structure.

Question

↓

Observation

↓

Explanation

↓

Underlying philosophy

↓

Practical takeaway

This structure should remain consistent across every brand whenever appropriate.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Media Company aims to become a trusted global publisher of Japanese knowledge.

Our brands may differ in subject matter, but they all share one purpose:

To help people understand not only what Japanese people do, but why they do it.

Understanding culture begins by understanding everyday life.
